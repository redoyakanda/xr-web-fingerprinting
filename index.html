Participant notice

This experiment records webpage interaction metadata while you complete standardized tasks, including focus transitions, navigation-key categories, clicks, and coarse scroll behavior.

It does NOT record:

  • typed text, passwords, or form values
  • screen-reader speech
  • microphone audio or camera data
  • motion sensor data, head pose, hand tracking, or eye tracking
  • precise pointer trajectories
